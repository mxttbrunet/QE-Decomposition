NOTES
==============================================================================
- QAOA-decomp: analytic p=1 QAOA reduction across minimum node cuts,
  stops when the cut no longer separates the graph (or |K|>6, |V|<=2);
  final reduced graph solved with analytic QAOA. Deterministic (seeded).
- MERGE: minimum-node-cut reduction with Gurobi+least-squares reweighting,
  an optional shot-based QAOA merge when |K|>3, stops at |V|<=M (M=5);
  final reduced graph solved by brute force. Merge step is stochastic,
  so MERGE ratios may vary by ~0.01-0.1 between runs.
- Timings exclude the exact-optimal solve; they cover decomposition
  plus the final small-graph solve only.
